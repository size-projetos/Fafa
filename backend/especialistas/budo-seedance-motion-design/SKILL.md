## 1. What this vertical optimizes for

Motion design for tech sells **invisible value** — software, data, productivity, connection. Unlike products you can hold (fashion, food), software has to be made visible through UI, data viz, and conceptual metaphor.

Three audiences must be served:
- **Engineers** want to see the actual product working.
- **Business buyers** want to see the outcome (metrics improving, time saved).
- **End users** want to see themselves succeeding with the product.

Quality vocab from Base §3 leans on §3.3 (Unreal Engine / cinema 4D) for 3D segments and §3.8 (Apple-keynote-grade) as production-value anchor.

## 2. Motion design hook library (12 hooks)

1. **Data Visualization Explosion** — Numbers/metrics explode outward with particle trails. Best for analytics, BI, metrics dashboards.
2. **UI Morphing** — Interface elements shift, reshape, transform. Best for design tools, dev platforms.
3. **Device Appearing from Void** — Smartphone/laptop materializes from darkness or blur. Premium, cinematic.
4. **Code-to-Product Transformation** — Code lines animate, compile, morph into final product. Best for dev tools.
5. **Metric Counter Rapid Climb** — Numbers count up rapidly (users, revenue, time saved). Creates urgency and proof.
6. **Glitch-to-Clean Transition** — Brief digital glitches resolve into pristine UI. Problem-to-solution storytelling.
7. **Hand Interaction with Device** — Human hand taps, swipes, gestures on floating device. Immediate relatability.
8. **Neon Glow Activation** — Dark interface suddenly illuminated by neon glows, lines, particles. Cyberpunk energy.
9. **Isometric Camera Zoom** — 3D isometric view zooms/pans to reveal product. Sophisticated spatial storytelling.
10. **Particle Burst from Logo** — Brand logo explodes into particles that form product interface. Branded momentum.
11. **Split-Screen Feature Stack** — Multiple screens slide in from sides, stacking like cards. For multi-feature products.
12. **Light Beam Scan** — Light beam sweeps across interface revealing details. Dramatic reveal.

## 3. Motion Design Philosophy for Software

**Show the product, don't describe it.** Tech videos that explain in voiceover are forgotten. Tech videos that *show* the click-to-result are remembered.

**Speed of comprehension is the metric.** Every second of unclear UI animation = a scroll. Test mentally: a non-technical viewer should understand the value within 5 seconds.

**Hierarchy of information.** What does the viewer NEED to know? What's nice-to-have? What's noise? Cut to bone.

**Premium = restraint.** Apple, Linear, Notion don't overload their videos with effects. Restraint signals confidence and quality.

**Audio carries the rhythm.** Even silent video implies audio. Specify the audio events (click, swoosh, chime) — they shape pacing.

## 4. Visual Style Library for Tech Ads

**Dark Premium.** Deep navy / black base, neon cyan/gold accents, isometric 3D, ray-traced reflections. Apple/Linear/Stripe.
**Clean Minimal.** White or very light gray base, single brand color accent, lots of whitespace, flat 2D motion. Notion/Slack/Figma.
**Cyberpunk Tech.** Black base with magenta/cyan/lime neons, glitch transitions, retro-futuristic typography. Gaming/dev tools.
**Glassmorphism.** Translucent UI cards floating on gradient backgrounds, frosted glass effects, subtle drop shadows. Apple Vision Pro lineage.
**Brutalist Tech.** Bold typography, high contrast, primary colors, rough edges. Modern design tools (Linear, V0, Vercel).
**Pastel Tech.** Soft pastels (lavender, mint, peach), playful motion, rounded corners. Consumer apps targeting younger audiences.
**Data-Forward.** Charts and metrics dominate, dark navy + green/red financial palette, professional. Analytics, BI, fintech.
**Hand-Drawn Tech.** Sketched lines, doodles, whiteboard aesthetic. Explainer videos for complex concepts.

## 5. Product Showcase Techniques

**Floating device.** Phone/laptop suspended mid-frame, no environment, content on screen is the focus.
**Hand-held device.** Realistic hand holding device with on-screen action. More human, less premium.
**Isometric 3D.** Device at 45° angle showing dimensionality, often with connected ecosystem elements.
**Multiple devices in sync.** Phone + tablet + laptop showing the same content syncing. Cross-platform story.
**Screen replacement (on-set).** Real environment with composited screen content showing the product.
**Camera through screen.** Camera "enters" the screen, immersing the viewer in the UI.
**Comparison split.** Old way vs. new way side-by-side. Problem/solution clarity.
**Macro on detail.** Extreme close-up on a single UI element (button, input, animation) to showcase craft.

---

## 6. Typography & Motion Graphics Guide

**Type animation:** kinetic typography (letters move and assemble), reveal masks (text appears via wipe), scale-in (small to readable), color shift (gray to brand color when emphasized).

**Iconography animation:** icons morph between states, fill animations, line-drawing reveals, micro-bounce on appearance.

**Chart/data animation:** lines drawing themselves, bars rising, pie segments unfolding, numbers ticking up.

**Cursor/pointer animation:** stylized cursor moving deliberately, clicks indicated by ripple, hover states shown.

---

## 7. Transition Library

**Match cut on shape.** Object in one scene becomes object in next via shared silhouette.
**Smooth morph.** Element shape-shifts between UI states.
**Whip pan.** Fast horizontal sweep, motion blur.
**Cross-dissolve.** Standard opacity blend; restraint.
**Push.** New scene pushes old off-frame.
**Slice/wipe.** Geometric reveal (vertical line, diagonal, radial).
**Particle dissolve.** Old scene dissolves into particles that reform as new scene.
**Glitch.** Brief digital corruption between scenes.

## 10. Motion-design-specific mistakes (in addition to Base §5)

- **Over-effected.** Every transition glitching/exploding/morphing reads as desperate. Restraint signals confidence.
- **UI screens unreadable at speed.** A dashboard shown for 0.6s with 10 elements visible is useless. Slow down or show fewer elements.
- **No real product reference.** "Show a dashboard" without screen designs attached produces generic-looking fake UI. Always pass UI screenshots via `medias`.
- **Generic motion design clichés.** Random particles for no reason, swooshing lines without anchor, kinetic typography without purpose. Each effect should serve clarity.
- **CTA missing or unclear.** Final 2 seconds must have a clear next-step verb. "Learn more" is weak; "Start free", "Try it", "Get started" are concrete.
- **Voiceover scripts in the prompt.** Seedance doesn't generate voiceover; specify implied audio (clicks, whooshes), not narration.
